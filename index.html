The road

Keep running

The knight never stops moving. The dead come from the dark on both sides, and every swing has to land on the move.

The blade

Timing over button mashing

Aim each swipe at head, body or legs. Early, perfect or late changes what happens next. Parry, clinch, and keep the chain alive.

The chapel

The Devourer

At the end of the road, a four-phase fight against Morvael, the demon waiting in the chapel. Before you face it, you pull a fallen hero’s sword from the dead. That is the blade that ends it.

Capture six souls and the duel is yours. Collect 666 and the contract is complete. Every soul makes you stronger, and a little less human.

The studio

Angry Bao is an independent worldbuilding studio. We build story-driven worlds, then everything that lives in them: games, collectibles and merch.

Scroll

What we make

Every world starts with a story. Dead of Knight, Evergreen and Triple Six are the first three, a run-and-slash game for mobile, a tactical tabletop RPG and a card and dice game. More worlds will follow.

Then everything around them. Each world grows its own product lines: apparel, cards, collectibles, stories, and whatever the world needs next.

Community first. We build in the open, test with real players and only ship what we would buy ourselves.
